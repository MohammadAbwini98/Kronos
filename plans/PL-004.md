Yes. The project already has a good base for this: signal validation currently uses EMA20/EMA50, RSI14, MACD histogram, ATR/ATR percentile, ADX/trend strength, VWAP, volume z-score, and support/resistance distance across higher timeframes.

The best next step is not to feed lots of indicators directly into Kronos yet. I’d add them first as an execution-validity filter/scorer after the forecast, so they decide whether a predicted LONG/SHORT is actually tradable.

High-value indicators to add:

Trend regime

EMA slope
EMA20/EMA50 distance
ADX strength
Helps avoid taking SHORTs in strong uptrends or LONGs in strong downtrends.
Volatility regime

ATR percentile
Bollinger Band width
Realized volatility over 12/24/48 candles
Helps block signals during extreme chop or unstable spikes.
Momentum confirmation

RSI slope, not just RSI level
MACD histogram direction/change
Rate of change over 3/6/12 candles
Helps verify the forecast direction has current market support.
Liquidity / cost safety

Spread percentage
Volume z-score
VWAP distance
Candle range vs spread
Helps avoid valid-looking predictions that cannot beat execution cost.
Support/resistance risk

Distance to nearest support/resistance
Recent swing high/low
Breakout vs rejection state
Helps avoid LONG right under resistance or SHORT right above support.
Candle quality / price action

Body-to-range ratio
Wick rejection ratio
Consecutive candle direction
Engulfing/impulse candle flags
Helps detect weak signals or reversal risk.
Best design:

Keep Kronos forecast quality separate.
Add an execution_validity_indicators snapshot to each signal.
Add component scores like:
trend_validity_score
momentum_validity_score
volatility_risk_score
liquidity_cost_score
support_resistance_score
price_action_score
Learn over time which indicators actually correlate with executed trade wins/losses.
So yes, we can add them, but the clean path is: indicators validate whether to execute a forecast, not whether the forecast itself was correct. That separation will make the win rate diagnosis much clearer.
